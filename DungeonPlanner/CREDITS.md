## Forever's new dungeons (Data/ForeverDB.lua)

Hand-researched in November 2026 from public player guides (Mobalytics, Method, Warcraft
Tavern, wow.gg, warcraft.wiki.gg) and written in our own words: boss order, entrances,
quest givers and spots, and loot names. No text or files were copied from those sites.

## Dungeon floors (Data/Floors.lua)

Each dungeon floor's world boundaries and map picture file numbers. These are Blizzard's own
game data (the client's UiMapAssignment and UiMapArtTile tables). The numbers - and only the
numbers - were taken from the export that ships with the ForeverDungeonRoutes addon, with
permission, and converted by `tools/gen_floors.py`. Thanks to its author.

## Quest rewards and the Tier 0.5 chain

The reward details on quest tooltips (which items are a choice, XP, money, reputation;
`Data/QuestExtra.lua`) and the quest IDs and order of the Tier 0.5 upgrade chain
(`Data/Tier05.lua`) come from CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0). The step hints are our own words.
What the game itself shows for a quest is remembered and always used first.

## Map backgrounds and the Start marker

The map pictures in Media/DJ are repainted on Dungeon Planner's own parchment
(`tools/clean_maps.py`): the decorative sketches, banners and torn frames are gone and only
each dungeon's layout is kept. The old-map artwork around it (aged paper, ink contour lines,
compass rose, title scroll and corner pieces) is drawn from scratch by `tools/map_art.py`. 

The maps of WoW Forever's new dungeons (Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation
Site, City of Dalaran and its Underbelly, The Drowned City and Atal'Byzal, Krol'dok
Stronghold, Shaper's Terrace, Alcaz Prison and Blackmaw Hold) were created by
**Santiago Reyes**, from his project Atlas de Azeroth: Forever - https://sreyesmaps.com.
He offers the low-resolution versions free to the community; each of his maps carries the
line "Map created by Santiago Reyes · sreyesmaps.com". Please support his work there. The green-flag
Start marker (Media/Start.tga) was drawn for Dungeon Planner.

## Dungeon maps (Data/DJMaps.lua, Media/DJ)

The dungeon maps - map pictures, the patches laid over the game's own map tiles, floors and boss
spots - are from the Dungeon Journal addon by Exehn, used with the author's permission at the
user's request (`tools/gen_djmaps.lua`). Boss portraits (the creature model for each boss)
and Forever boss levels (Data/BossArt.lua) come from the same addon. Thanks to Exehn.

## Walking network (Data/WalkNet.lua)

The walking network the arrow follows - the paths around mountains, cliffs and water on
Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms - is from the Dungeon Journal addon by Exehn, used with
the author's permission at the user's request. The data comes from Blizzard's own game
world. The route search that uses it (Services/Route/WalkNet.lua) was written for
Dungeon Planner. Thanks again to Exehn.

## Map drawing (MapDraw.lua, Libs/LibDeflate.lua)

Drawing on the maps and sharing them follows the Raid Assignment Manager's map page (the user's
own addon). Compression uses LibDeflate by Haoqian He (zlib License), bundled in Libs/.

## Forever loot and quest rewards

Boss drops, drop chances and quest rewards for Forever's new dungeons were researched from
kami-labs.fr's WoW Forever instance pages (read from the beta client). When the game's own
Encounter Journal has a boss's loot, the addon shows that instead, with real item tooltips.

## Forever's loot changes in classic dungeons (Data/ForeverLoot.lua)

Boss and creature drops WoW Forever changed in 16 classic dungeons (Ragefire Chasm through
Zul'Farrak redone; new items added in Blackrock Depths, Maraudon, Scholomance and
Stratholme). Researched from public loot tables (item IDs as listed by timesaver.gg,
cross-checked with Method, Icy Veins and warcraft.wiki.gg) and generated by
`tools/gen_forever_loot.py`. The recipe, pattern and blueprint drops, and a few items those
tables missed, come from Exehn's Dungeon Journal (2.1.7.3), used as a reference with the
user's go-ahead.

## Not used

Apart from the Dungeon Journal maps, boss portraits and levels credited above (used with
Exehn's permission), no code, data or artwork from other WoW: Forever addons is included.
The quest warnings in Data/QuestWarnings.lua are written in our own words; the facts
(which quest steps happen outside the dungeon) were checked against classic quest data.
The mountain-pass seeds for the travel arrow follow the Horde road into Ironforge that
Dungeon Journal's route uses.

## Secrets

The Secrets page (Data/Secrets.lua) lists only routes marked "Solved" on WoW Handbook
(https://wowhandbook.com/secrets/). The steps and coordinates were researched there and
rewritten in our own words. Thanks to WoW Handbook and the players who solved them.

## Translations

German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Korean and Chinese
(Simplified and Traditional) interface text (Locales/), plus the secrets' summaries and rewards
and the quest warnings (Locales/Notes/), were written for Dungeon Planner. Item names and the
individual secret steps stay in English. Corrections from native speakers are welcome.
